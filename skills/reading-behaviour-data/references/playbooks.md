# Behaviour playbooks

Each section is one to-do's steps. A to-do on the What to do page names its section by id.

### PB-BEHAVIOUR-SCROLL-SHORT — Most people never reach the bottom

**When:** On the busiest pages, few visitors scroll as far as the content that matters most.

**Do:**
1. Find where the price, the main action and the contact options sit on those pages.
2. Move the one that matters most into the part of the page most people still see, as one change, so the next run shows whether it moved.
3. Shorten or cut what sits above it if it does not help the visitor decide.

**Check after:** Clicks on the moved elements on the next run.

### PB-BEHAVIOUR-EXIT-PAGE — Visits end on pages that should lead somewhere

**When:** Pages that are meant to move people on are where many visits end.

**Do:**
1. Start with the busiest of those pages.
2. Give it one obvious next step, the single thing the visitor should do after reading it, placed where the reading stops.
3. Compare it with a similar page that people leave less often.
4. If its exit share does not move, the cause may be who arrives rather than the page: check what each source does after it lands.

**Check after:** The exit share of that page on the next run.
