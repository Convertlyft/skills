# Sales, enquiry and sign-up playbooks

Each section is one to-do's steps. A to-do on the What to do page names its section by id.

### PB-CRO-ATTENTION-OFF-ACTION — Attention lands away from the main action

**When:** Clicks and taps on a page gather around elements other than its main action.

**Do:**
1. Open the heatmap for the page on the device with the most traffic.
2. Find the main action (buy, book, contact) and see where it sits against the clicks.
3. If it is not where the attention is, move it there. If it is there and still gets nothing, make it look like a button and say what happens after it. Make one of these changes at a time.

**Check after:** Clicks on the main action on the next run.

### PB-CRO-LANDING-VS-LANDING — A page converts below similar pages

**When:** One page turns visits into results less often than pages of the same kind on the site.

**Do:**
1. Open the page next to a similar page that does better.
2. Compare the offer above the fold, the main action and what the visitor must do to act.
3. Change one of those three, not all of them, so the next run shows which one moved.

**Check after:** That page's rate, against the same comparison, on the next run.

### PB-CRO-PAGE-WINS — A page converts above similar pages

**When:** One page turns visits into results more often than pages of the same kind.

**Do:**
1. Read the page and write down what it does that the weaker pages do not.
2. Copy one of those things to the weakest similar page and leave the rest alone.

**Check after:** Whether the copied change travels: the weaker page's rate on the next run.

### PB-CRO-SLOW-MONEY-PAGE — A page that earns is slow

**When:** A page that carries many results loads slowly for a share of its visitors.

**Do:**
1. Load the page on a throttled mobile connection and watch what arrives last.
2. Start with the heaviest images, scripts and fonts above the fold.
3. Make sure the main action is usable before everything else has finished loading.

**Check after:** Load time for that page, and its rate, on the next run.

### PB-CRO-NO-CONTACT — Busy pages show no clicks on the contact option

**When:** High-traffic pages show attention on the page but little or none on the contact or main action.

**Do:**
1. Find the contact option on the page picture for the busiest device.
2. If it sits far down the page, try it higher up.
3. Make it a clear, tappable control that says what happens next.

**Check after:** Clicks on the contact option on the next run.

### PB-ACQ-SOURCE-WINS — A source converts better than the site average

**When:** One source turns visits into results clearly better than the site does overall.

**Do:**
1. Find out what is being said about the business there: the post, listing, partner page or campaign sending the visits.
2. Look at which pages those visitors land on and what they do next.
3. Do more of what works there before spreading effort across weaker sources.

**Check after:** Whether that source keeps its lead as its volume grows.

### PB-FUNNEL-BIGGEST-LEAK — The widest drop in the funnel

**When:** One step loses more people than any other step in the same funnel.

**Do:**
1. Walk that step yourself on a phone, start to finish, as a first-time visitor would.
2. Note everything that asks for effort or trust at that step: extra fields, surprise costs, a slow load, an unclear next button.
3. Fix the single biggest one first, so the next run can show whether that step moved.

**Check after:** Step completion for that step, read against the period before the change.

### PB-ACQ-AI-TOOLS — AI tools send people here

**When:** Visits arrive from a link in an AI answer (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot) or an AI tool's own browser, and either end in results more often than the site's average, or come in numbers and none end in a result.

**Do:**
1. Find where they land: `cvl_page_conversions` with `source` `ai_tools` lists those pages with their visits and results.
2. On the page most of them land on, put the facts an AI answer quotes (price, place, hours, what is included) and one way to buy or get in touch in the first screen.
3. When they already do better than average, link that page from the home page so more visits reach it.

**Check after:** Results from AI-tool visits on the next run, against their visits.

### PB-CRO-PRODUCT-SHORT — A product is added to the cart less often than the others

**When:** One product's page is viewed often but added to the cart far less often, per page view, than the shop's other products together.

**Do:**
1. Give its page what the product that does best shows in its first screen on a phone: the price, delivery and the Add to cart button.
2. Change one of the three first, so the next run shows which one made the difference.

**Check after:** Visits that add it to the cart, per page view, on the next run.
