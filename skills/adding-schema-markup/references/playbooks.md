# Schema playbooks

Each section is one to-do's steps. A to-do on the What to do page names its section by id.

### PB-SCHEMA-NONE — Pages carry no structured data

**When:** Pages that describe the business or what it sells carry no structured data.

**Do:**
1. Add JSON-LD that describes what each page actually is, starting with the pages about the business and its services or products.
2. Use only facts that are visible on the page.
3. Check each page in Google's Rich Results Test.

**Check after:** Pages with structured data on the next crawl.
