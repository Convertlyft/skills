# Replay playbooks

Each section is one to-do's steps. A to-do on the What to do page names its section by id.

### PB-REPLAY-WATCH-FRICTION — Recordings show visitors getting stuck

**When:** Recordings of recent visits carry stuck-spot markers.

**Do:**
1. Open the first recording at the first stuck-spot marker on its timeline (a rage click, a dead click or an error).
2. Read what happened just before and after it to see what the visitor was trying to do.
3. Check a second recording before deciding it is a pattern.

**Check after:** Stuck-spot markers on that page on the next run.
