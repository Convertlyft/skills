# Drop playbooks

Each section is one to-do's steps. A to-do on the What to do page names its section by id.

### PB-DROP-SOURCE-RATE-FELL — A source converts less than it did

**When:** One traffic source sends as many visits as before but fewer of them end in a result.

**Do:**
1. Write down what shipped between the two periods: a release, a price change, a new ad or audience, a changed landing page.
2. Open a few recent visits from that source and compare where they land with the visits from the earlier period.
3. If the landing page changed, start there. If nothing on the site changed, the change is in who that source now sends: look at the campaign or referrer behind it.

**Check after:** The same comparison on the next run, with the change you made noted on the timeline.

### PB-FUNNEL-STEP-FELL — A funnel step fell from its usual level

**When:** A step that usually completes at a steady rate completed noticeably less this week.

**Do:**
1. Check what changed on the page behind that step: a release, a price, a form field, a new ad sending different people.
2. If nothing on the page changed, compare who arrived: split the step by source and device to see which group moved.
3. If it went up instead, write down what changed while it is still known, so the next drop has something to be read against.

**Check after:** Give it one clear week after any edit before comparing again.

### PB-CONV-TRACKING-BROKE — Results stopped being recorded

**When:** The step before a result carries on (checkouts started, forms started) and no result has been recorded for far longer than the site's own rate explains.

**Do:**
1. Put the tag and its result event back on the page that recorded the last result. On Shopify, turn the pixel back on in Settings › Customer events.
2. If that page changed (a new checkout app, a new thank-you page), put the event on the page people now see after a result.

**Check after:** Results are recorded again within 7 days.
