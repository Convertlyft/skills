# Paid traffic playbooks

Each section is one to-do's steps. A to-do on the What to do page names its section by id.

### PB-ACQ-CAMPAIGN-NOTHING — A campaign brings visits but no results

**When:** A paid campaign sends a steady stream of visits and none or almost none of them convert.

**Do:**
1. Point the campaign's links at the page that sells what it offers: same offer, same product, same wording, with a way to buy or get in touch in the first screen.
2. Narrow the targeting and search terms behind it to people who can buy, and pause it until the page matches before raising its budget.
3. Evidence, not the fix: one recording of a visit from the campaign shows what the visitor met after landing.

**Check after:** Results from that campaign on the next run, against the visits it sent.

### PB-ACQ-AD-WRONG-PAGE — An ad lands on a page that does not sell

**When:** Paid visits land on a page that informs or closes (a blog post, About, Legal, a thank-you page) instead of one that sells or takes an enquiry.

**Do:**
1. Change the ad's link to the page that sells what the ad offers. When the to-do names a page that already turns paid visits into results, use that page.
2. If no page sells that offer yet, make one: the offer, the price or how it works, and one way to buy or get in touch in the first screen.

**Check after:** Paid visits to that page on the next run (they should fall), and results from the page the ad now points at.
