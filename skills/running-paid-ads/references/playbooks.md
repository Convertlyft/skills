# Paid traffic playbooks

Each section is one to-do's steps. A to-do on the What to do page names its section by id.

### PB-ACQ-CAMPAIGN-NOTHING — A campaign brings visits but no results

**When:** A paid campaign sends a steady stream of visits and none or almost none of them convert.

**Do:**
1. Open a few of those visits in the recordings and watch what the visitor did after landing.
2. Check that the landing page is the page the ad promises: same offer, same product, same wording.
3. Check the targeting and search terms behind the campaign for traffic that was never going to convert.
4. Pause or narrow the campaign before raising its budget.

**Check after:** Results from that campaign on the next run, against the visits it sent.
